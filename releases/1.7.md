SZNS 1.7 makes settings quieter and easier to scan.

* Choose colors in one compact row. Open **Edit…** for individual colors, then Apply or Cancel.
* Click a scene’s name to preview or mix it. Scene rows have fewer competing controls.
* General focuses on app behavior and Quiet Hours. **About SZNS** holds update checks, source code and the welcome guide.
* Scenes and Color palettes keep separate searches.
* Blank SVG bulbs are rejected, imported bulb padding stays transparent, and out-of-range color channels are clamped before saving.

Download **SZNS-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and move **SZNS.app** to Applications. The universal app supports Intel and Apple silicon on macOS 14 or newer. See [Opening a downloaded app](https://github.com/Tekwiz17/SZNS#opening-a-downloaded-app) for the locally signed download.
