SZNS 1.3 adds **Lantern Festival** and **Koi Pond** to the scene collection. Paper lanterns glow as they rise, and koi leave small ripples behind them. Both designs are also available as menu bar and Dock bulbs.

**Quiet Hours** in Settings → General hides decorations on a daily local-time schedule. For a meeting or screen share, choose **Pause Decorations** from the menu bar for 15, 30, or 60 minutes. Your scene and individual layer switches are preserved.

Download **SZNS-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and move **SZNS.app** to Applications. The app supports Intel and Apple silicon on macOS 14 or newer. This build is locally signed. For a downloaded copy you trust, the README covers Apple's per-app **Open Anyway** option.
