**Snow settles on your desktop.** Falling snow builds shallow banks along each display’s bottom edge. Wind moves the landing spots, fresh flakes replenish the banks, and older deposits melt. Turn off **Let snow pile up** for falling flakes alone. Rain also makes brief ripples where drops land.

**Settings are easier to scan.** Choose Scenes or Color palettes in Presets. Bulb design, size, pattern and colors are up front. Expand the motion and cable controls for finer adjustments.

The scene mixer now describes the layers you’ll actually apply. Launch at Login refreshes after returning from System Settings, and setting desktop Amount to zero removes the unused overlay.

Download **SZNS-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and move **SZNS.app** to Applications. The universal app supports Intel and Apple silicon on macOS 14 or newer. See [Opening a downloaded app](https://github.com/Tekwiz17/SZNS#opening-a-downloaded-app) for the locally signed download.
