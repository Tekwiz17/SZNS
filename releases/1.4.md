SZNS has a new four-petal liquid-glass icon and a matching monochrome menu bar mark.

* **Mix scenes.** In Presets, click the eye beside a scene, select the Dock, menu bar or desktop layers you want, and preview them alongside your current setup. Apply the mix and save it under a new name.
* **Sea Glass.** Tumbled-glass bulbs and translucent rising bubbles share a palette of mint, sea blue, apricot and pink. The new bulb and effect also work in Shortcuts.
* **Less idle work.** Turning off all three decorations now puts the overlay polling timer to sleep. Year bulbs update when the calendar year changes.

Download **SZNS-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and move **SZNS.app** to Applications. The universal app supports Intel and Apple silicon on macOS 14 or newer. For the locally signed download, see [Opening a downloaded app](https://github.com/Tekwiz17/SZNS#opening-a-downloaded-app).
