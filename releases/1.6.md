Falling leaves now settle in shallow piles along the bottom of your desktop. Choose **Desktop → Leaves → Let leaves gather** to turn the piles on or off.

Carved **Jack-o’-lanterns** glow near the lower corners with Flying Bats and Floating Ghosts. Add them to Leaves if you like, or turn them off for any of the three effects. Each effect remembers your choice. Amount controls how many pumpkins appear. Reduce Motion keeps their candlelight steady.

Both details stay behind your apps. Switching piles on or off keeps airborne particles in place, and animations now use an uptime clock to avoid a daily jump.

Download **SZNS-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and move **SZNS.app** to Applications. The universal app supports Intel and Apple silicon on macOS 14 or newer. See [Opening a downloaded app](https://github.com/Tekwiz17/SZNS#opening-a-downloaded-app) for the locally signed download.
